Skin-mounted UV sensing

How can a skin-compatible sensor make UV exposure readable without an electronic display?

Photochromic and UV-absorbing materials support reversible color changes in epidermal UV sensors. The study combines materials and device design for naked-eye, skin-color-specific monitoring of UV exposure.

UV exposure → reversible color response → visual readout

My contribution: Epidermal sensor development within the collaborative materials and device study.

Soft robotics & microfabrication

PUBLISHED EXAMPLE · THIRD AUTHOR

Millimeter-scale EEM motor fabrication

How can an elastomeric electromagnetic mechanism be fabricated at millimeter scale?

The EEM motor combines an elastomeric structure, magnet and coil. The smallest motor shown on a fingertip measures 2.4 × 2 × 1 mm. Its miniaturized fabrication is distinct from the larger autonomous crawling and swimming robots demonstrated by the team.

Photolithography and molding → millimeter-scale motor → actuation

My contribution: I fabricated the millimeter-scale EEM motor using MEMS microfabrication.
